What this is

Stiff Competition is six characters and their stories: Major Wood, The Rear Admiral, Hammerin’ Hanky, The Grip, Slick Willy and The Velvet Python. The characters come first, and everything else serves them.

Three used action figures started it. A pound for the lot, bought on a Wednesday afternoon on the way to the pub. The photos were phone snaps taken at the pub, and the figures had ended up in poses so lifelike that the idea arrived fully formed. Six original characters grew out of them.

The work is organised into four series. Every piece is original digital art, registered to whoever buys it, permanently and publicly. Once a piece is gone, it is gone.

The four series

Each series puts the same six through a different set of conditions. Pieces are numbered continuously within a series.

Blueprint Mugshot Edition

430 pieces

Portraits shot against the same board, front and profile. The largest series, and the one closest to how a toy line gets documented before it ships.

Championship Handlers Series

96 pieces

The six out of uniform and into sport: golf, football, and a long ride through the mountains. Three sub-series sharing one sequence.

Travel the World Series

92 pieces

The premise is a package holiday. What comes back is closer to postcards, sent from somewhere that exists only in the series.

The Sovereign Assets Series

40 pieces

The smallest and most formal of the four. Full-length studio portraits at the top grade, one character to a piece, with the whole frame given to him.

How it gets made

One person, start to finish

The artwork, the shop and the code, learned from scratch over months by one person, with the build documented as it goes.

Every piece is made deliberately, one at a time, then graded and numbered. The grade describes work already done.

About

Stiff Competition is six original characters built like the action figures you’d have found on a shelf in 1994: chunky sculpts, loud colours, and names that tell you immediately what kind of humour you’re dealing with.

Out of the box, they are dealing with what everyone deals with: work, admin, group chats, the small indignities of being an adult. Heroic design, unheroic life. New work appears as an ongoing series across social media and the store.

The humour is period-correct to 1994, an era less careful than ours, and it is meant fondly.

Please note: the backstory is original fiction. The characters are presented as prototypes from a 1994 toy line by a company called RIGID-DYNE, crated up and forgotten for thirty-two years, written for the brand, fan wiki included.

The six

Major Wood

Infantry squad commander. Always standing, on the grounds that softness is a security risk.“I’m up and I’m staying up!”

The Rear Admiral

Naval officer and precision logistics specialist, at sea so long he forgot how land-lubbers talk.“Requesting immediate boarding!”

Hammerin’ Hanky

Construction worker. Believes a solid foundation is the key to a job well done.“I’m here for the big project. Heavy tool ready.”

Slick Willy

Wrestler and the greasiest grappler in the game. Slippery by design.“Coming in slippery!”

The Velvet Python

Wrestler and master of coiled positions. Soft texture, rigid as they come.“The Python is hungry.”

The Grip

The squad’s powerhouse. Full-body contact and manual handling.“A firm hold on the situation.”

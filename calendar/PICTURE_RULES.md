## 1. Scale
- Full body, head near the top edge, boots near the bottom edge: figure fills about 90% of the picture height. Check after every picture: head-to-feet 85% or more of frame height, else redo.
- Same height every month for every character. A prop never covers the head or shortens him.

## 2. Placement
- Figure sits left third, centre or right third, rotating month to month. The set's key props fill the open side.
- Different stance each month: lean, stride, twist, flex.

## 3. Pose and face
- Pin-up pose for a calendar: chest out, shoulders back, hip set to one side, one hand on the hip or in a flex.
- Face: sexy smile or smouldering look straight into the camera, one eyebrow raised. Never a scowl or stern face, never looking away at a prop.
- Skin has a light oil sheen. Top opened, pulled down or off in his own outfit; Velvet Python keeps his snakeskin top on.

## 4. Colour
- Each month has two bold colours that contrast with the character's outfit (table below). Saturated, glossy, toy-box look.
- Coloured rim lights on the figure's edges, glowing bokeh lights and sparkle, bright oversized shiny props.
- No brown, grey or beige sets.

## 5. Semi-real toy world (the Christmas ad look)
- Figure is a glossy plastic action figure with visible joints; props and set are toy-scale miniature, real materials, real lighting, shallow depth of field.
- NO display stand, NO base disc: boots on the set floor with soft contact shadows.
- No readable writing, text or letters anywhere in the picture. Captions are typeset text on the page.

## 6. Innuendo
- Safe: the caption and the prop carry it; the picture stays PG-13 (no nudity below the waist, no explicit acts).

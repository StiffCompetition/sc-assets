WHATSAPP — ANDROID AND IPHONE

WhatsApp keeps sticker packs inside a separate sticker app, so the files go in
through one of those. It takes about two minutes per pack.

1. Install a sticker maker app. Sticker Maker and Sticker Maker Studio are both
   free and both work on Android and iPhone.
2. Open it and choose Create a new sticker pack.
3. Name it "Stiff Competition — Reactions" (or Moods for the second pack), and
   put whatever you like as the author.
4. Add the stickers from the pack folder in this download.
5. Tap Add to WhatsApp and confirm.

The pack then appears in your WhatsApp sticker tray, right next to the emoji
button, ready to send.

Do the two packs separately. WhatsApp allows 30 stickers in a pack, which is why
there are two of them.
